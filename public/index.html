✿ PATRICIAFOUR WILD WORLDS

Three lives.
Four wild worlds.

Pick a level, then your characters. Play alone with two computers, share a device with up to three people, or create a room code for other devices.

Lives: Purple ghosts cost 1 life. Yellow ghosts are fast and cost 1. Blue ghosts cost 2. Patricia takes all remaining lives. Gaps cost 1 life. Computers follow the same rules.

Stars: Each star gives a temporary speed boost. Collect another to extend it. Stars never affect lives.

Controls: Solo: ↑ / ↓ or W / S. Shared device: P1 W / S, P2 ↑ / ↓, P3 I / K. Touch: each player has their own arrow buttons. P pauses local games.

Goals: Survive 2 minutes to the lighted road tunnel, 4 minutes to the night forest, or 5 minutes to space. Sea mode has no finish: it gets darker and faster until everyone is out.

Change lanes to avoid potholes, missing treetops and cloud gaps. Tree characters swing automatically; birds flap while airborne. Reach the finish for confetti. Everyone earns a medal: finishers rank ahead of eliminated racers. First eliminated gets third place, second eliminated gets second, and last eliminated gets first when nobody finishes.
